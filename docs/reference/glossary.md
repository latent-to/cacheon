# Glossary

**Arena**
: Validator-owned hardware, workload, policy, and service boundary for one
  qualification domain.

**B**
: The complete-engine replay of the frozen incumbent evaluation stack, paired
  window by window with C. After a speed PASS the still-loaded incumbent also
  generates the selected stock-drift controls for the quality gate.

**C**
: A complete isolated candidate engine equal to the incumbent stack except for
  exactly one registered target delta.

**Contribution**
: A bounded semantic improvement associated with one registered target. It may
  exist as hostile proposal content or as reviewed source whose selected bytes
  remain bound to the crowned payload digest.

**Crown**
: A transactional settlement result backed by one complete audited PASS
  qualification; historical crowns keep their paired evidence. It advances an
  evaluation stack; it does not ship code, and reward credit does not require it.

**Evaluation stack**
: Content-addressed arena incumbent that may contain hostile crowned proposal
  references. It exists only inside the referee system.

**Futility margin**
: Optional sealed fraction of a statistical speed grade. A complete first lane
  orientation that reads more than this much slower than the incumbent fails
  without running the swapped orientation. It can end a grade early, never pass one.

**NO_DECISION**
: Qualification outcome indicating that valid authority could not be
  established. It is neither candidate failure nor success.

**Orientation**
: One assignment of the incumbent and candidate engines to the two physical lanes.
  A statistical speed grade splits its paired windows between two orientations,
  swapping lanes and booting both engines fresh in between, and scores the
  geometric mean of the two pooled cost ratios.

**Pristine T**
: Candidate-free, untimed reference engine and hidden quality authority. It
  grades sealed trajectories after all candidate engines are destroyed.

**Proposal**
: Content-addressed untrusted submission admitted for a specific target. It is
  not installed source, a crown, or a release.

**Qualification**
: The complete registered evidence procedure: a sealed current speed
  schedule (the paired replay, speed policy 17);
  registered eager audit A; candidate teardown; pristine T quality grading;
  exact-schema decision; and retained evidence.

**Reproduction pending**
: Historical state after one authoritative PASS. A current controller accepts
  the retained complete audited PASS on restart without another qualification.

**Reward group**
: One arena digest and one incumbent stack digest. A PASS earns V1 credit only if
  it beats the best earlier rewarded PASS in its group by the required margin
  (1.5% under speed policy 17); kernel and cache PASSes share a group.

**Slot**
: Validator-owned execution ABI inside the pinned engine. Slots define how a
  component is invoked; targets define how a contribution is identified and
  rewarded.

**Target**
: Smallest registered semantic reward identity. A normal proposal resolves to
  exactly one target: `forward_pass` or `prefix_cache`.

**Weight intent**
: Journaled desired chain publication derived from the pure global emissions
  projection. It remains pending until confirmed by later chain observation.
